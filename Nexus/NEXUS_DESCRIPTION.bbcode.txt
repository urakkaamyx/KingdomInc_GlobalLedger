[size=5][b]Global Ledger[/b][/size]

Track live currency earnings, operating expenses, and item production across your entire kingdom from a dropdown on the top Mod Bar in Kingdom Inc.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Rolling average toggle on UI:[/b] Click the [b]Rolling Avg[/b] button in the top-right header bar to toggle rolling averages on and off without having to open the config menu. The button turns Gold when active.[/*]
[*][b]Mod Config menu integration:[/b] In the Mod Config menu, you can enable [b]Rolling average[/b] and customize the [b]Entry Count[/b] slider (2 to 30, default 3). Enabling rolling averages smooths out short-term spikes in rate calculations based on your selected unit (e.g., 3 entries over 3 seconds when [b]sec[/b] is selected, or 3 entries over 3 minutes when [b]min[/b] is selected).[/*]
[*][b]Basic | Adv mode toggle:[/b] Switch between a streamlined summary and the detailed hierarchical breakdown using the [b]Basic[/b] and [b]Adv[/b] buttons in the top-right corner.
  [list]
  [*][b]Basic mode:[/b] Lists out [b]Currency Net Collection[/b] and [b]Item Net Production[/b] in a clean, scrollable overview ([b][Icon] [Name] +/-XXX[icon] / {sec|min|hour}[/b]), sorted by [b]Tier -> A-Z[/b]. Items and secondary currencies without production activity in the window are cleanly hidden unless [b]Show All[/b] is toggled on.
  [*][b]Adv mode:[/b] Retains the full breakdown (Currency / Item > Container > Building > Operating Costs) and defaults to everything collapsed so you can cleanly drill into specific containers or production chains.
  [/list][/*]
[*][b]Rate scaling (sec / min / hour):[/b] Click [b]sec[/b], [b]min[/b], or [b]hour[/b] in the header bar (defaults to [b]min[/b]) to scale all rates to per-second, per-minute, or per-hour across all rows and denominations.[/*]
[*][b]Currencies tab (Adv):[/b] Shows every unlocked currency's rolling net rate ([b]Earned - Spent[/b] over the last 60 seconds). Gold is written out in full Gold, Silver, and Copper denominations with native coin symbols (1g = 100s = 10,000c); other currencies keep their native units. Expand a currency root to inspect containers, individual buildings, and [b]Operating Costs[/b] (icon-only currency rows).[/*]
[*][b]Production tab (Adv):[/b] Follows the same hierarchy for unlocked items, sorted by [b]Tier (Tier 1–12) -> A–Z[/b]. Displays each item's rolling net production rate ([b]Produced - Consumed[/b]). Items with zero activity in the last minute are hidden by default; click [b]Show All[/b] in the header bar to show or hide zero-activity unlocked items. Expand an item root to inspect containers, individual buildings, and [b]Operating Costs[/b] (both consumed input items and spent currencies as icon-only rows).[/*]
[*][b]Container & workspace grouping:[/b] Container rows display [b][ContainerIcon] | [ProductionIcon] [ContainerName][/b] using the game's native green container icon, a [b]|[/b] separator, and the overlapping icon sticker nearest the frame's top-left corner (or its building's production icon when no sticker is placed), alongside the cleaned container name (with TextMeshPro tags such as <size> stripped). Duplicate container names are disambiguated with stable [b]_1, _2[/b] suffixes to prevent jumping around. Nested or overlapping frames assign each building to the smallest frame once; buildings outside frames appear under their workspace's [b]Uncontained[/b] group.[/*]
[*][b]Collapse All & independent expanders:[/b] Click any row or its left arrow to expand or collapse its children without moving the camera. Click [b]Collapse All[/b] in the header bar to collapse all open roots, containers, buildings, and operating cost sections on the active tab so you can drill down one level at a time.[/*]
[*][b]Hover highlight & dedicated View button:[/b] Hovering a container or building row highlights the object in the current workspace without panning. Live container and building rows provide a right-aligned [b]View[/b] button that switches workspaces if needed, centers and highlights the target for five seconds without closing the dropdown.[/*]
[/list]

[b]How the accounting works[/b]
Rates reflect actual activity recorded in one-second buckets over the last 60 real elapsed seconds. During the first minute of observation, the header displays a collecting indicator and shows the accumulated sample without extrapolating short bursts. Pausing the game produces no new activity while older observations age out.

Shop, Restaurant, Requisition Office, and Disenchanter collection-tray changes are combined with wallet credits so income is recognized when earned and collecting a tray never counts twice. Pure routing nodes and storage pass-throughs are excluded from item production and consumption. Deleted buildings retain their recorded observations under [b]Removed buildings[/b] until the 60-second window expires.

[b]Requirements and installation[/b]
1. Install the dependencies listed in this page's Requirements section ([b]BepInEx 5[/b] and [b]Foundation 0.6.0 or later[/b]).
2. Close Kingdom Inc.
3. Extract the ZIP into the game folder beside KingdomInc.exe.
4. Confirm the file is at [b]BepInEx/plugins/GlobalLedger/GlobalLedger.dll[/b].
5. Launch the game.

[b]Saves and removal[/b]
Accounting history is session-local and adds no custom buildings, research, or save data. Close the game and delete [b]BepInEx/plugins/GlobalLedger[/b] to remove the mod.
